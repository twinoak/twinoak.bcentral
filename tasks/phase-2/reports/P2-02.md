## Decisions taken

- **The switcher is a popover-sized card, not a one-sentence popover.** The task moves
  `IncidentBar`'s whole job (pick, create with the drill checkbox, close/reopen with a reason,
  export, print) behind the incident chip, and the document's popover is "a title line, a
  sentence, one action". The switcher uses the same `Popover` component and `.popover` rules
  (360 px, the shadow) with a stacked body: the select, the opened-by line, the reason row,
  Export / Print, the create form. Nothing else in the app has this job, so the journal page
  lost it entirely rather than keeping a second copy.
- **Chip states → tints.** Disconnected and blind are danger (P1-09 mapped blind to the
  danger dot; the document's danger is "radio gone"); connecting is warn with a hollow dot.
  Storage: a folder write that failed is danger (broken), permission and no-persistence are
  warn (a decision), no folder with persistence promised is grey (nothing is at risk, but the
  chip says what is missing). The task says "hidden when persistent and a folder is set" —
  read as `persisted() === true && mirror ready`; a folder in `needsPermission` or `error` is
  set but not writing, so the chip shows.
- **Chip details hide under 90 rem.** Measured at 1280 px the bar's content (wordmark,
  incident with its open duration, three tinted chips, five Danish nav links, operator,
  language, ?) is ~1400 px; nothing short of dropping the navigation fits. So "· heard 3 min"
  and "· open for 2 h" show from 1440 px up and live in the popover below that; "· closed"
  shows at every width. With that, the calm state fits one row at 1280 (measured 56 px) and a
  crowded one wraps the navigation group to a second right-aligned line — one surface, one
  rule under it, 90 px instead of 56. Flagged as a deviation; the document's "one 56 px bar"
  holds whenever the chips are quiet.
- **The nav is not a `.tabs` strip.** `.tabs` has a bottom rule; the bar's own rule is that
  line. The links are `.tab` with `aria-current="page"` (P2-01's follow-up), 40 px tall.
- **Esc through the shortcut registry, not a keydown on the popover.** One path, asked before
  any form's cancel because it registers later; Esc inside the switcher's name field closes
  the switcher (Esc is allowed in text fields by `lib/shortcuts.ts`).
- **The `?` card is 26 rem, not 22.5.** At 360 px every shortcut line wraps and the card
  outgrows 720 px; at 26 rem with the table in 14 px it holds. Placed under the ? button
  (top right) rather than centred, since it is the one card that behaves like a popover.
- **Toasts for the four confirmations the task names** (saved, exported — journal and
  register CSV, printed) and the incident created/drill created (it closes the switcher, so
  the old inline notice had nowhere to be). Register saved/bound stays inline (P2-04).
- **Minute floor on the open duration** (`max(60 s)`, a 10 s tick): a bar that counts
  seconds flickers; the handover page keeps its seconds.
- **`theme-color` white**: the installed app's title bar continues the bar; it cannot follow
  the drill tint (a static manifest value) — noted below.
- **Sizes in rem** as P2-01: the bar is `min-height: 3.5rem`, the chips 1.5rem, so the three
  text sizes scale the whole bar (the largest capture wraps, as every bar would at 21 px).
- The P1-09 rule of 40 px targets is relaxed for the bar's chips (24 px, the document's
  chip); the nav links keep 40 px.
- Candidates for `docs/decisions.md`: the switcher as a popover card; chip details from
  1440 px up and the two-line fallback; `theme-color` as `--surface`.
