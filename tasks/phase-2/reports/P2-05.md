Every capture against `docs/design.md` §Forbidden and §Components:

§Components — the deviations found, with what was done:

1. **Register form showed "Skriv en betegnelse." on opening** (`register-1280x720` before the
   fix): a field error on an untouched field, which the document's field says is for an invalid
   control. *Fixed*: the error waits for blur or a save.
2. **Handover print's "To" line carried the current operator** (`print-handover-A4` before the
   fix) because the field is prefilled with that name. *Fixed*: blank line unless a different
   name is typed or a handover was done.
3. **Register at 1280 with the add card open**: the toolbar wraps to two lines (the five
   buttons and the search field beside a 22.5 rem card) and the Person column wraps names to
   two lines, so rows are 56 px, not 32. Not fixed (a layout choice: hide the key column or the
   toolbar labels while the card is open — P2-04's follow-up stands). Follow-up.
4. **Handover strips wrap to three lines in the half-width cards at 1280** (sent · delivered
   · age), P2-04's known follow-up. Not fixed.
5. **The journal's "no radio" message under the compose form repeats the bar's radio chip**
   (every journal capture): the document wants the inline message "next to the thing it is
   about" and a chip in the bar; both say the same thing all evening while no radio is
   connected. Not a rule breach; a question below.
6. **The queue's title chip "På pause: ingen radio" also repeats the bar** (`queue-1280x720`).
   Same question.
7. **The largest size** (`journal-largest-1280x720`): the side cards cut the message text to
   "#…" and the compose card takes a third of the height; P2-03's follow-up (a 64 rem

8. **The print journal's text column** is half the sheet and the Radio column a sixth, so a

   12 pt; a `max-content` radio column would give the text more. Follow-up.
9. **The paper's row list** (`print-handover-A4`): the quiet radios print one name per 32 px
   line, 22 lines; a two-column list on paper would halve it. Follow-up, low.

## Decisions taken

- **`playwright-core`, not `playwright`.** `require("playwright")` did not resolve (nothing in
  the project or globally; the machine's `ms-playwright` cache holds an older Chromium from
  another project). The task then says add it as a devDependency. `playwright` downloads three
  browsers on every `npm ci` — the deploy workflow runs `npm ci` on every push to `main` —
  while `playwright-core` is the same API without the download; the script launches
  Playwright's Chromium when `npx playwright install chromium` has been run, else the Edge on
  the machine (`channel: "msedge"`), else Chrome. Candidate for `docs/decisions.md`.
- **One stylesheet still**: the document's `.doc` block and the paper rules went into
  `components.css` rather than a `print.css`, since the document says one component sheet and
  the paper rules are the components' own (a chip on paper is still the chip).
- **Status words on paper by rule, not by markup**: the journal print already wrote words; the
  handover print renders the screen's lists, so the paper block strips every chip to its word
  instead of a second markup path. The strip's `·` separators stay.
- **The print pages' sheet on screen** is a `.doc` of its own (card-like, no `.card`): a card has
  a 20 px title and one purpose; a document has an h1 and a `.dl`. On paper both lose their edge.
- **The no-incident state of the print view** opens the switcher like the handover's, rather
  than linking to the journal; one behaviour for "no incident" everywhere.
- **The captures are the calm state**: the clock confirmed through its popover (session state,
  so after every reload) and persistent storage promised by an init script, since headless
  Chromium refuses it and an installed app on a laptop is granted it; the radio is
  disconnected as the task says. The crowded bar (three tinted chips, two lines at 1280) is
  in P2-02's report; the first-run setup card is in P2-03's `dev-dist` captures and has no
  committed capture here (the task's list does not name it). Follow-up.
- **Fixed clock** (`page.clock.setFixedTime`, 2026-10-28 19:45 Z): the relative times on the
  screens are stable across runs, so a re-run only differs where the app changed. The one
  moving string is "Bygget" under Settings → About, which is the build time.
- **Danish** for the set: the staff's language, and what the P2-02 … P2-04 captures used.
- **Extra files beyond the task's list**: `queue-compose` (the compose card is otherwise in no
  capture), `settings-full` and `styleguide-full` (a 720 px cut of a scrolling page shows a
  fifth of it), the three A4 PDFs (the acceptance's A4 check) and the three A4-wide PNGs
  (PDFs do not render in every reviewer's viewer; the PNG is the same paper media at the
  same width). `journal-largest` and `journal-contrast` at both sizes.
- **The PDF page count** is read from the PDF's `/Pages … /Count` object and printed by the
  script; a cross-check counting `/Type /Page` objects gives the same (3, 2, 3).
- **`__bcentral` typed through `declare global`** in `main.ts`, so `svelte-check` sees the hook
  and nothing else in `src/` knows about it.
- **Prettier** was run on every new and touched file; `app/README.md` stays unformatted as
  before. Every changed file is LF.
- Candidates for `docs/decisions.md`: playwright-core with a channel fallback; the paper
  rules in the component sheet; screenshots as committed review material.
