## Decisions taken

- **Rows as a subgrid.** `.rows` is the grid and each row lays its cells on the list's
  columns, so time, who, body and strip line up down the list as a table's columns do.
  Chromium has had subgrid since 117 (the app is Chromium-only). The style guide's sample
  list is unchanged by it.
- **The strip column is capped at a third of the list** (`fit-content(33%)`). A shared column
  takes the widest strip of any row; uncapped, a batch row's four counters pushed the body to
  a few words at 1280 and to one letter per line at the largest size. With the cap a
  three-state strip ("sendt 20.26 · leveret 20.27 · roger 20.29") stays on one line at 1280
  and a batch's counters wrap to two. Flagged below.
- **Row actions on a line of their own, not an overlay.** Hidden until hover / focus-within /
  expanded, as the task says; shown, they add a 28 px line under the row (compact quiet
  buttons) rather than overlaying the strip, which is what the operator reads. The row
  grows on hover; moving the pointer down the list stays monotonic (the growth is below the
  pointer), so nothing flickers.
- **The operator has no column.** A slip is time | who | body | strip; who recorded it is in
  the time cell's tooltip and on every line of the history. The print view keeps its
  operator column.
- **Corrections are lines, not rows.** A correction is a 14 px `.msg` under its entry (there
  is no `.msg-neutral` class: `.msg` is the neutral variant, as `.chip` is, P2-01). It is
  not focusable and has no actions: a correction of a correction, and Correct on a history
  line, which the old table offered, are gone (the data model allows both). Follow-up.
- **The compose line.** Via, Recipient, a one-row textarea that grows (`field-sizing:
  content`, Chromium 123+; before that a one-row textarea that scrolls), Send; the byte
  counter on the 14 px meta line under it with the prefix sentence and the key. The compose
  card is padded `--s-4` top and bottom (the card's `--s-5` at the sides), its tabs sit
  `--s-2` above the form, and the gap to the list is `--s-3`: ten one-line rows under the
  card at 1280 × 720 need the card at 168 px, and the document's paddings gave 192. A
  deviation from `.card`; flagged below.
- **The list is the scroller.** The layout is `calc(100vh - 3.5rem - 2 * var(--s-5))` tall,
  the compose card is fixed-size and the list takes the rest; the side column scrolls on its
  own. The page never scrolls at ≥ 60 rem, except by the height of P2-02's second bar line
  when it wraps (the 3.5 rem is the bar's calm height). Under 60 rem the page scrolls and the
  list keeps P1-09's 60 vh cap.
- **Both forms mounted under the tabs**, the inactive panel `hidden`: the shortcuts' handlers
  stay registered, typed text survives, and N / A / a reply switch the tab through
  `onreveal` before focusing.
- **The setup card asks for the name; the clock and the radio can wait.** Start is disabled
  until a name is typed; the clock box and the radio buttons act at once and have their chips
  in the bar for later ("Can wait" under the radio buttons). Saving the name shows the Saved
  toast, as Settings does; the card's going is the real confirmation. The gate goes to the
  journal, not settings, so the first screen is the one with the card; Settings stays
  reachable and still sets the name.
- **Pending is warn.** A message nobody has handed to the radio or the courier needs a hand
  (the style guide already showed it so); waiting (sent, the receipt out) is warn with an
  outline dot like the bar's connecting chip; delivered / roger are told apart by the dot
  (P2-01's strip decision kept).
- **Direction as characters, not icons**: `→` / `←` in `--ink-2` before the name, with the
  visually hidden word; an icon is an action or a state (P2-01), and this is neither.
- **Side column 320 px**, the document's own px (P2-01 put sizes in rem; the column width is
  the one place the document writes px on purpose, and at the largest size a 20 rem column
  would be 420 px of a 1280 px screen). The who column is capped at 12 rem with an ellipsis.
- **"Mark"** for the manual-status opener (the task's word; the form's buttons are "Mark
  sent" …), Danish "Markér".
- **Prettier**: run on the new files and on the touched files that were formatted on `main`
  (`ManualEntryForm`, `OpenItemsPanel`, `Timeline`), and on `JournalPage.svelte`, which was
  unformatted on `main` but is rewritten whole here; `app/README.md` stays unformatted. Every
  changed file is LF.
- Candidates for `docs/decisions.md`: rows as a subgrid with a capped strip column; row
  actions on a line under the row; the list as the scroller at ≥ 60 rem; pending = warn.
