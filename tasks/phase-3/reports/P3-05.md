## Done

Any journal line can become a *point* (da: *punkt*): the open row offers Open point (a
one-field form with the title prefilled from the line's text), then Close point (a required
reason) and Reopen point; the line carries a small glyph after the time; the open points are
a card in the journal's side column above Open items and the first list on the handover page;
the print ends with a block of them. Nothing is stored on a point: its state is the latest
`closed` / `reopened` status change that refers to it, derived on every reload like a
message's status. A point is never a row.

- **`store/types.ts`**: `EntryType` gains `point`.
- **`store/journalView.ts`**: `PointView` (the point, its title through `currentText`, its
  origin entry, `state`, `openedAt`, `closedAt`, `closedBy`, `reason`, `changedAt`),
  `pointsFor(entries)` (every point oldest first; the state from the latest closed / reopened
  status change by status.ts's ordering, anything else referring to a point ignored),
  `pointOf(points, entryId)`, `openPoints` (oldest first), `closedPoints` (newest closed
  first); `buildTimeline` drops `point` entries (their status changes were already dropped
  with every status change that refers to no message), so the list, `findRow`, the open items
  and the print rows are what they were.

  reason })` — a required reason, refused when the point is closed (`isPointOpen`);
  `reopenPoint(store, point, { operator })` — refused when it is open; `PointError`. Every

## Decisions taken

- **"Point" is the English word**, as the task says; "follow-up" appears only in prose.
- **A point is never a row** of the journal or of the print, as a correction is not one
  (P3-02): the origin line carries the glyph, the open row the point line and the action,
  the card and the handover list the points. `buildTimeline` skips `point` entries; their
  `closed` / `reopened` status changes were already skipped (a status change that refers to
  no message and not to the incident). The print block is the task's "block at the end".
- **The glyph is a plain character with two shapes**, `◆` open in `--accent` and `◇` closed
  in `--ink-2`, in the time cell at the time's size — like the direction arrows, not an icon
  (an icon is an action or a state the bar names), and never colour alone (high contrast and
  paper keep the shape). The tooltip is "Open point: ‹title›" / "Closed point: ‹title›", so
  the state reads in words too.
- **The point action is the first button on the action row** (Open point · Retry · Mark ·
  Correct): the task's *Why* is the incoming call that needs a place to say "this is open",
  so it leads the line. The forms replace the action row like Mark and Correct do.
- **The title is prefilled from the line's text without a message's "#n "** (`bodyOf` after
  `currentText`), cut at 80 characters at a word boundary with no ellipsis: a title is the
  operator's to edit, not a quotation. One line: whitespace collapsed.
- **A batch row's point is its first message's**, as the task says; the first recipient's
  row inside the open batch therefore shows no glyph and no point button (the batch line
  has them), while every other recipient can carry its own point. `P` on a recipient row
  acts on that recipient's message. Flagged under *Open questions*.
- **`openedAt` after a reopen is the reopening's time**: the age on the cards counts from
  when the point last became open, not from its first opening; the point's own position in
  the lists stays its creation (oldest first by the point entry, as the task's "oldest
  first" reads). `changedAt` is what "since last print" compares.
- **A correction of a point corrects its title** (`currentText`), though no screen offers
  Correct on a point today; it keeps "every screen reads a text through currentText" true.
- **The Points card's rows are jump buttons with Close on a line under them** (a button
  cannot sit inside a button; the open-items card's rows are jump buttons too), with one
  sheet rule for the inset of a `.row-actions` / `.row-form` under a `.row-button` row. The
  handover's list is on the journal's `Row` and opens on Close, as its other lists do.
- **The handover's points card spans the width above the 2 × 2 grid** with its list capped
  at 30 vh: five cards in a 2 × 3 grid at 720 px would leave each about 150 px, and the
  to-do list is the one that is read first. The P2-04 test's `repeat(2, minmax(0, 1fr))`
  columns stay.
- **`P` opens the row first** when its action needs a form (Open point, Close point), so the
  form has a place; Reopen point writes at once from a closed row too. `P` is nothing on a
  row that cannot carry a point.
- **`journal.open.title` (da) is now "Åbne ordrer"**: it was "Åbne punkter", which beside the
  new "Punkter (1 åbne)" card named two cards *punkter*; "ordrer" is the handover's word for
  the same list. One existing string, Danish only; flagged for the human review of the
  Danish strings below.
- **The store refuses a point on a point or on a status change**; the screens never offer
  one there (the incident's own status rows and an orphan correction do not get the button).
- **`journal.type.point` was not added**: nothing reads a point's type as a word (a point is
  never a row, never in the print table).
- **Prettier** run on every touched file that was formatted on `main` and on the five new
  ones; `app/README.md`, `store/index.ts` and `store/types.ts` were unformatted on `main` and
  were left so; every changed file is LF. **The screenshot set was regenerated whole** (the
  counts under *Verify*).
- Candidates for `docs/decisions.md`: a point is never a row; the glyph as a two-shape
  character in the time cell; the point action first on the action row.
