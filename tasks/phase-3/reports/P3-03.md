## Done

The status of an outgoing message is a fixed-width column at the right of every row: three
slots — sent · delivered · confirmed — one symbol each, always in the same place, under three
13 px labels stuck to the top of the list. No time is shown in the list; the times are the
slots' tooltips and the row's history. The print view still prints the status words with
their times.

- **`lib/statusSlots.ts`** (new, pure): `statusSlots(strip, transport)` → three `Slot`s
  (`name`, `shape`, `tint`, `at`, `text`, `attempt`, `byHand`) off the message's strip (the
  current run, as before) and its transport: reached → filled `ok` (confirmed: `check`);
  given up → `cross` `danger` in the first slot not reached, the slots before it filled;
  sent without ack → `clock` `warn` in the delivered slot with the latest attempt; else
  `hollow` `neutral`. `byHand` is true on every slot of a message on a non-mesh transport
  (text-protocol.md: the operator sets those statuses by hand). `countSlots(counters)` →
  a count per slot (`n`, `total`), `ok` once every recipient reached the state, `neutral`
  until then.
- **`lib/journalText.ts`**: `slotText(slot, inflightText)` — "Delivered 12.21", "Not yet
  delivered", "Not yet delivered: waiting (attempt 2/3)" (the outbox's own progress text
  while it holds the message, as on the strip), "Given up 12.30 · no ack after 3 attempts",
  and "… · by hand (reason)" on a manual status; `countSlotText` — "23 of 25 delivered".
  `stripText` is unchanged and is now the print view's alone.
- **`store/journalView.ts`**: a `StripItem` carries its transition's `text` (the reason or
  detail), for the tooltips; an `OpenItem` carries the message's `strip`, so the open-items

  timeline does).
- **`components/SlotSymbol.svelte`** (new): the five shapes as inline SVG on the icon set's

## Decisions taken

- **A slot is 4 rem, the column 12 rem, not the task's "`--s-7` × 3 ≈ 7.5 rem".** The two
  numbers in the task disagree (3 × `--s-7` is 9 rem), and neither holds the header: at 13 px
  "Bekræftet" measures 63 px, "Delivered" 55, "Confirmed" 61, so a 40 or 48 px slot would
  have labels wider than the slot under them, which is no header. 4 rem (64 px) holds every
  label in both languages at every text size, scales with the font, and at 1280 px takes
  the same room the capped strip could. Measured in `journal-largest-1280x720.png`: the
  labels fit at 21 px.
- **The open-items card packs the slots (1.5 rem each) and has no header.** The card is
  320 px; 12 rem would leave nothing for the text. The symbols are the same, the tooltips
  say what the labels say, and the journal's header is on the same screen. This answers the
  task's open point: the labels are everywhere the full column is — the journal and the
  handover's open and given-up lists — and not on the side card.
- **"By hand" is the transport, not the entry.** A status on a non-mesh message is the
  operator's by the contract; on a mesh message `roger` and `given_up` can be manual too,
  but nothing in the data marks them apart from the radio's (a manual roger carries a reason
  where the radio's carries the incoming id; telling them apart by whether the text is an
  id would be a heuristic). Their tooltips say the status and time; the reason is in the
  history. Flagged under *Follow-ups*.
- **The cross goes in the first slot not reached**, which the task names as "delivered or
  confirmed"; given up before anything was sent (no radio connected) puts it in the sent
  slot, since that is the slot not reached. The tooltip carries the given-up reason.
- **A batch's count is `ok` only when every recipient reached the state**, `ink-2` until
  then; the task's "coloured like the symbol would be when all are done" was read as that
  condition. The given-up count sits under the three counts at the right rather than beside
  them: the column is fixed at three slots.
- **The in-flight words stay, in the tooltips.** The strip replaced its "waiting" segment
  with the outbox's progress text; the clock's tooltip does the same ("Not yet delivered:
  attempt 2/3, waiting for the receipt"), and a hollow sent slot says "Not yet sent: attempt
  1/3, sending" while the outbox holds a pending message. No symbol changes for in-flight
  before the first `sent`: the task's clock is for sent-without-ack.
- **The "answered ‹time›" links moved into the body.** The task says they stay "where they
  are in the who/body area"; they were in the strip cell, which is now the column. They sit
  after the text, as a reply's "↳ answers" mark does. The incident's closed / reopened chip
  that also sat in the strip is dropped; the body already says the word.
- **The handover's age moved after the time** (13 px `.row-meta`), since the strip it hung
  on is gone and the column holds nothing but slots.
- **The shapes are drawn in `SlotSymbol.svelte`, not added to `lib/icons.ts`**: the icon set
  is stroke paths with no fill, a filled disc and a cut-out check are not; and the set is
  the bar's and the buttons' vocabulary. The symbol is on the icon grid and stroke all the
  same, so it reads as one family.
- **The header is a component of its own** (`StatusColumnHead.svelte`) with a children
  snippet, so the journal's "N new" chip rides on it and the handover lists render the
  labels alone; the task names one component and gets two plus the symbol.
- **`StripItem.text` and `OpenItem.strip`** are view-model additions; the contract and the
  store are untouched ("out: the data model").
- **Prettier** run on every touched file (all were formatted on `main`) and on the five new
  ones; every changed file is LF.
- Candidates for `docs/decisions.md`: status as a fixed column of symbols with no times in
  the list; "by hand" means a non-mesh transport; the column's width is set by its labels.

## Follow-ups

- [ ] A manual `roger` or `given_up` on a mesh message is not marked "by hand" in the
      tooltip (the transport is the rule); the reason is in the history. If the staff want
      it, the roger's text being an entry id in the journal is the only thing that tells
      the two apart — decide whether that is a rule worth writing into the contract.
- [ ] The body column at 1280 px is narrower than before by about 60 px when the who column
      is wide ("← Hans · Telefon"): two-line bodies are more common. P3-04's at-rest rows
      could cap the who column lower, or the time column could drop its seconds.
- [ ] The open-items card cuts the text hard at the largest size (three packed slots take
      94 px of 272); a shorter form of the item (the `#n` alone) or hiding the time there
      would give the text room.
- [ ] The queue's title counter chips and the style guide's chip samples keep the old dot
      look beside the new symbols; one vocabulary for "a state on a chip" would be tidier
      (the task said nothing more on the queue).
- [ ] The slots' sentences are tooltips and `aria-label`s only; a keyboard user reads them
      through the row's accessible name, not on hover. P3-04's open row could list the three
      sentences.
- [ ] No component test harness (P1-02 follow-up stands): the alignment under the labels,
      the sticky header and the tooltips are checked on the sources and in the captures.
